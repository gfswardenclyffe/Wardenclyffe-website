# Wardenclyffe roadmap

1. Refine the four service divisions, their dedicated interactive detail views and legal qualifications.
2. Review Work descriptions for truthfulness and client confidentiality before publication.
3. Approve contract templates by legal jurisdiction and product specifications; confirm prices/taxes, consumer rights and professional engagement boundaries.
4. Deploy Cloudflare Worker payment backend in sandbox only, connect Stripe test keys, PayPal Sandbox credentials, D1 and Resend. Test signed webhooks and verified delivery.
5. Set up private approved product documents, a receipt/support/retry pathway, refund policies and international VAT/tax logic.
6. Only then plan a monitored live deployment with merchant-of-record, conflict checks and professional authorizations.
7. Confirm every social profile link before publishing it; update the footer with verified profiles.
