03 / TEXAS NOTARY PUBLIC

Notarial services, properly understood.

Explore traditional Texas notarizations and online notarizations where an individually commissioned and authorized Texas notary is available. Texas notaries are not the equivalent of Mexican notarios públicos. This page does not claim that Wardenclyffe or any named individual currently holds a Texas notary commission.

IN-PERSON · TEXAS

Acknowledgments & jurats

Identity verification, signature acknowledgment and jurats or oaths for eligible documents, subject to applicable Texas rules, presence requirements and the notary's commission.

Availability, eligibility and statutory fees to be confirmedAsk about availability ↗
REMOTE / ONLINE · IF AUTHORIZED

Online notarization & related acts

Remote online notarization by an appropriately commissioned online notary, if available; certified copies only where allowed by law, eligible affidavits, powers of attorney and document authentication questions. Apostilles are issued by the competent authority, not the notary.

Separate online commission and technology requiredRequest a notarial inquiry ↗
Important distinction

A Texas notary public cannot practice law or offer legal advice solely by virtue of being a notary, may not advertise as a “notario público,” and may not notarize documents in ways prohibited by Texas law. Services will be listed as available only after the relevant commission and authorization have been verified.
