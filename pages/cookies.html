November 8, 2026 · Wardenclyffe, Inc.

This Cookie Policy (“Policy”) explains how Wardenclyffe, Inc. and its subsidiaries and affiliates (“Wardenclyffe”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) use cookies and similar technologies on www.wardenclyffefirm.com and associated websites, online stores, applications, and digital services under our control (collectively, the “Website”).

This Policy forms part of our broader privacy disclosures and should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions of Service.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website.

They allow websites to recognize browsers, retain preferences, maintain sessions, improve functionality, and understand visitor interactions.

Cookies may be temporary, lasting only during a browsing session, or persistent, remaining on your device until they expire or are deleted.

We may also use similar technologies, including browser local storage, pixels, and other technologies that collect or store information.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

A. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies support essential Website functions, including:

Website navigation and basic operations.

Authentication and account security.

Fraud prevention and system protection.

Shopping cart and checkout functionality.

Remembering cookie consent choices.

Where legally permitted, strictly necessary cookies may operate without optional consent because they are essential to providing requested services.

B. Functional Cookies

These cookies support enhanced Website features, including:

Remembering language preferences.

Saving optional personalization settings.

Maintaining user-selected display preferences.

Supporting certain interactive Website functions.

Where consent is required, these cookies will only be activated after authorization.

C. Analytics and Performance Cookies

Analytics cookies help us evaluate how visitors interact with the Website.

Information may include:

Pages visited.

Time spent on particular pages.

Approximate geographic region.

Browser type and device characteristics.

Navigation patterns and performance data.

We use such information to evaluate Website performance, identify technical issues, and improve the user experience.

Where legally required, analytics cookies will remain disabled until consent is obtained.

D. Marketing and Advertising Cookies

Where enabled, marketing cookies may be used to:

Measure advertising performance.

Understand interactions with promotional campaigns.

Support personalized advertisements.

Limit repeated advertising.

Evaluate marketing effectiveness.

These technologies may involve third-party advertising providers.

Where prior consent is legally required, these cookies will not be activated without authorization.

3. First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are placed by Wardenclyffe-controlled websites or systems.

Third-party cookies may be placed by external providers whose technologies are integrated into the Website.

Depending on the services and features enabled, third-party providers may include payment processors, analytics platforms, social media services, embedded content providers, and other technology vendors.

Such providers may process information under their own privacy notices.

The specific cookies, providers, purposes, and retention periods may vary between the Website, online store, and associated applications. Where required, this information will be made available through the applicable Cookie Settings or cookie disclosure.

4. Cookie Consent

Where required by applicable law, Wardenclyffe requests your consent before activating non-essential cookies or comparable technologies.

When visiting our Website, you may be offered the following options:

Accept All Cookies: Authorizes the use of all available cookie categories, including optional analytics, functional, and marketing cookies.

Reject Non-Essential Cookies: Allows strictly necessary cookies while disabling optional categories.

Cookie Settings: Allows you to review and individually select optional cookie categories.

Consent must be provided through an affirmative selection where required by law.

Simply continuing to browse the Website does not constitute consent to non-essential cookies.

5. Managing and Withdrawing Consent

You may change or withdraw your cookie preferences at any time through the Cookie Settings option available on the Website.

You may also configure your browser to block or delete cookies.

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies through browser settings may affect account access, checkout processes, and other essential Website functionality.

Withdrawing consent prevents future processing dependent on that consent, but may not automatically remove information previously collected.

6. Cookie Retention

Cookie retention periods depend on their functions.

Session cookies are generally deleted when the relevant browsing session ends.

Persistent cookies remain until their configured expiration date or until removed by the user or browser.

Wardenclyffe endeavors to configure cookie retention periods in accordance with operational requirements, data minimization principles, and applicable legal obligations.

7. Personal Information and Cookies

Certain cookies and tracking technologies may collect identifiers or information that qualifies as Personal Information under applicable privacy laws.

Such information is processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Where applicable, visitors may exercise privacy rights concerning information collected through cookies, including rights to access, request deletion, or opt out of certain processing activities.

8. Browser Privacy Signals

Where required by applicable law, Wardenclyffe will recognize and honor qualifying browser-based privacy preference signals relating to applicable opt-out rights.

Such signals are distinct from general browser settings that block or delete cookies.

9. Updates to This Cookie Policy

Wardenclyffe may revise this Policy to reflect changes in Website technology, service providers, legal obligations, or data practices.

Updated versions will be published on the Website with an updated effective date.

Where required by law, material changes affecting consent will be accompanied by an appropriate notice or renewed consent request.
