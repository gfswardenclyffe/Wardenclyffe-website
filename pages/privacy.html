THE DOCUMENT / FULL TEXT

November 9, 2026 · Wardenclyffe, Inc.

Wardenclyffe, Inc. and its subsidiaries and affiliates (“Wardenclyffe”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respect your privacy and are committed to responsible personal data management.

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes how we collect, use, process, retain, protect, and disclose information relating to individuals (“Personal Information”) when they visit www.wardenclyffefirm.com, access our associated platforms, purchase products, communicate with us, or use our Services.

This Policy should be read alongside our Terms and Conditions of Service and Cookie Policy.

I. Collection of Personal Information

We may collect Personal Information directly from you, automatically through your interactions with our Services, or from third parties where legally permitted.

The categories of information we may process include:

Personal InformationPurpose of Processing
Full name, address, and date of birthIdentity verification, customer registration, service administration, and legally required documentation.
Email addressAccount management, customer support, transaction confirmations, and communications.
Telephone numberService notifications, customer support, and authorized communications.
Payment and billing informationTransaction processing, invoicing, accounting, and fraud prevention.
Government-issued identification or tax information, when requiredBusiness registrations, regulatory filings, compliance, and professional services.
Social media information voluntarily providedCustomer communications and optional account features.
Technical and usage dataWebsite security, functionality, analytics, diagnostics, and service improvements.
Cookie identifiers and online interaction informationSession management, preference storage, analytics, and advertising where applicable.
Documents and information provided in connection with ServicesPerforming requested professional, administrative, digital, or other contracted Services.

We endeavor to limit data collection to information reasonably necessary for the purposes disclosed in this Policy.

Where we need to process sensitive Personal Information, we apply any additional safeguards and consent requirements imposed by applicable law.

II. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

Wardenclyffe may process Personal Information for the following purposes:

1. Providing products and Services requested by customers.

2. Creating and managing user accounts.

3. Processing transactions and maintaining financial records.

4. Verifying identity and preventing unauthorized access.

5. Communicating regarding orders, accounts, and services.

6. Improving Website functionality and security.

7. Analyzing Website performance and user experience.

8. Complying with legal, tax, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

9. Protecting Wardenclyffe's legal rights and legitimate interests.

10. Delivering marketing communications and personalized content where authorized.

Where required by applicable law, processing will be based on an appropriate legal ground, including consent, contractual necessity, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests.

Where consent is required, you may withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing performed before withdrawal.

III. Storage and Retention of Personal Information

Wardenclyffe retains Personal Information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, contractual, regulatory, and recordkeeping requirements.

Retention periods may vary according to the type of information, the Services provided, and applicable law.

Personal Information may be stored or processed on systems located within or outside the United States.

When information is no longer required, Wardenclyffe will take reasonable steps to securely delete, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of the information, subject to applicable retention obligations.

IV. Sharing Personal Information with Third Parties

Wardenclyffe does not disclose Personal Information to unrelated third parties except as described in this Policy, as authorized by you, or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

1. Service Providers

We may share information with third-party providers that assist with payment processing, website hosting, electronic communications, cloud infrastructure, analytics, cybersecurity, and other business operations.

Such disclosures are limited to purposes reasonably related to the services performed by those providers and are subject to appropriate contractual or legal safeguards.

2. Subsidiaries and Affiliates

Personal Information may be shared among Wardenclyffe entities when reasonably necessary to provide requested Services, manage operations, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Professional and Administrative Services

Where required to perform a requested Service, information may be disclosed to authorized professional advisers, government agencies, registries, filing authorities, and other relevant recipients.

4. Analytics and Advertising Partners

Where applicable, third-party analytics and advertising providers may process information collected through cookies or similar technologies.

Non-essential tracking technologies requiring consent will be subject to your cookie preferences.

Certain disclosures involving advertising technologies may constitute a sale or sharing of Personal Information under applicable privacy laws, even where no monetary payment is exchanged. Where such laws apply, appropriate disclosures and opt-out mechanisms will be provided.

5. Legal Compliance and Protection

We may disclose Personal Information when necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, enforce agreements, protect rights, investigate fraud, or address legitimate security concerns.

6. Corporate Transactions

Information may be transferred or disclosed in connection with a merger, acquisition, corporate restructuring, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable safeguards and legal requirements.

V. Data Security

Wardenclyffe implements reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards designed to protect Personal Information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, and disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, authentication procedures, encryption where appropriate, system monitoring, and restrictions on the handling of confidential information.

However, no electronic transmission, storage system, or online platform can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and promptly notifying Wardenclyffe of suspected unauthorized account activity.

VI. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, and comparable technologies to provide functionality, remember preferences, assess performance, and support optional advertising activities.

These technologies may fall into the following categories:

Strictly Necessary Cookies: Support essential Website operations, security, authentication, and requested transactions.

Functional Cookies: Remember preferences and support optional personalized features.

Analytics Cookies: Help us understand Website traffic, performance, and visitor interactions.

Marketing and Advertising Cookies: May support advertising measurement, audience analytics, and personalized advertising where enabled.

Where applicable law requires consent, optional cookies will not be activated until the appropriate consent has been obtained.

You may manage your choices through the Website's Cookie Settings, including accepting, rejecting, or selectively enabling optional categories.

Rejecting optional cookies will not prevent access to the Website's essential functions, although certain optional features may be unavailable.

For additional information, please review our Cookie Policy.

VII. Marketing Communications

Wardenclyffe may send promotional communications when you have provided the consent required by applicable law or when another lawful basis permits such communications.

You may opt out of promotional emails through the unsubscribe mechanism included in those communications or by contacting us at info@wardenclyffefirm.com.

Opting out of marketing does not prevent us from sending necessary transactional, administrative, or legal communications.

VIII. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and applicable law, you may have the right to:

1. Request confirmation of whether we process your Personal Information.

2. Access Personal Information relating to you.

3. Request correction of inaccurate information.

4. Request deletion of eligible Personal Information.

5. Request a portable copy of eligible information.

6. Object to or restrict certain processing activities.

7. Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

8. Opt out of certain targeted advertising, personal data sales, or qualifying automated profiling.

9. Appeal certain decisions concerning privacy rights requests where required by applicable law.

We will respond to verified requests within the periods required by applicable law.

We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity or authority before fulfilling a request.

Certain requests may be denied or limited where legally recognized exceptions apply, including the preservation of records required for legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or contractual obligations.

To exercise your rights, contact: info@wardenclyffefirm.com

You may also use applicable Website privacy controls when available.

Where required by law, eligible browser-based privacy signals and authorized requests will be honored.

IX. Children’s Privacy

Our Website and general Services are not directed toward children under the age of thirteen (13).

Wardenclyffe does not knowingly collect Personal Information directly from children under thirteen for general Website marketing or account registration without any consent or authorization required by applicable law.

Where Personal Information relating to a minor is necessary for a properly authorized professional Service, it will be processed subject to applicable legal requirements and safeguards.

If you believe information has been collected from a child without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

X. International Data Transfers

Wardenclyffe may operate through affiliated entities, service providers, and infrastructure located in different jurisdictions.

Personal Information may therefore be transferred to or processed in countries other than the country in which it was originally collected.

Where applicable, we will implement safeguards required for international transfers, taking into account relevant data protection laws and contractual requirements.

XI. Cookie Consent and Preference Management

When you first visit our Website, you may be presented with a cookie consent notice explaining the categories of technologies used and their purposes.

You may choose to:

Accept all optional cookies.

Reject non-essential cookies.

Customize your preferences by category.

Strictly necessary cookies may remain active because they support essential Website functions.

Your cookie preferences may be stored to remember your selection.

You may change or withdraw your consent through Cookie Settings. Withdrawal will apply prospectively and does not automatically erase information lawfully processed before withdrawal.

Additional details appear in our Cookie Policy.

XII. External Websites and Third-Party Platforms

Our Website may contain links to external websites, applications, payment gateways, and third-party platforms.

Wardenclyffe does not control the independent privacy practices of these third parties.

We encourage visitors to review their privacy notices and cookie policies before providing Personal Information or interacting with external services.

XIII. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Wardenclyffe reserves the right to update this Policy to reflect changes in our business operations, Services, technology, or legal obligations.

Any revised version will be published on our Website with an updated effective date.

Where required by applicable law, material changes will be accompanied by additional notice or requests for consent.

XIV. Governing Law

This Policy shall be interpreted in accordance with applicable law, including the laws of the State of Texas where appropriate.

Nothing in this Policy limits mandatory privacy rights or protections available to individuals under applicable laws of other jurisdictions.
